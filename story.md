# Youth of the Future / شباب المستقبل

My own notes about the story. `python -m pipeline draft` turns them into `storyboard.draft.json`; I edit that by hand.

## Who, where, when
- Me: born in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, in 2001, to an Ethiopian family. Shown only as an anonymous illustrated boy
  (about 8 years old) and, at the end, as an anonymous young man (25). No real faces.
- My mother appears only as hands in the kitchen. My father appears only as a shadow / silhouette at the door.
- Place: our apartment in Riyadh, mostly the salon (living room) with an old TV and floor cushions; the kitchen behind it.

## What happened (true)
- At home we spoke Amharic. The home was warm: my mother cooking Ethiopian food (injera on the round griddle, shiro,
  red berbere spice), and we were always waiting for dad to come home in the evening.
- After school and on weekends I sat alone in the salon and watched Spacetoon, the Arabic children's channel.
  Its slogan was "سبيستون، قناة شباب المستقبل" (Spacetoon, the channel of the youth of the future).
- I learned Arabic mostly from the cartoons and especially from their opening songs: I sang along with every intro.
- Outside, I shared Saudi food (kabsa, sambousa and dates in Ramadan) with friends who sang the same songs.

## The turn and the ending
- Spacetoon called us "the youth of the future". Now it's 2026, I'm 25, and I made this short cartoon myself, with AI.
- Last lines (meaning): "They called us the youth of the future. They were right. Now I make the cartoons myself."

## Audience and feeling
- For: people who grew up in the Gulf / Arab world in the 2000s ("the Spacetoon generation"), especially children of
  migrant families (Ethiopians and others) who learned Arabic from TV.
- Feeling at the end: warm nostalgia, then quiet pride. Two homes, one childhood.

## Constraints (important)
- Do NOT depict any real cartoon characters, the Spacetoon logo, or any channel branding. The TV shows only abstract
  colourful light. The "planets" are our own invented, simple glowing planets, not the channel's designs.
- No text, letters or logos in any image.
- Style: warm, soft 2D illustrated look (gouache / storybook), warm lamp light at home, cool TV glow, consistent
  characters across shots.
- Narration: Modern Standard Arabic, simple and spoken, short sentences. English subtitles.
